# Vendor communication

## Replying to payment status queries

Vendors often ask when an invoice will be paid. Look the invoice up in Ledgerly and reply from the
AP mailbox. A good reply:

- references the vendor's invoice number and amount,
- states the current status in Ledgerly (Pending, Approved, Paid),
- gives the scheduled payment date if the invoice is Approved, or the payment date if it is Paid,
- is short and polite, and is signed "Accounts Payable, Northstar Retail".

Never share Northstar bank details or internal approver names in vendor emails.

## Emails to external addresses

Any email to an address outside `@northstar.example` is an external communication and is reviewed
before it is sent.

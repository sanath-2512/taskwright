# Ledgerly quick guide

Ledgerly is the AP system of record.

- **Invoices** lists every recorded invoice with its status. You can search by vendor or invoice number.
- **New invoice** opens the form for recording a vendor invoice.
- Each invoice has a record ID like `AP-000123` and a status:
  - *Pending*: recorded, awaiting approval for payment,
  - *Approved*: approved and scheduled for payment on the scheduled payment date,
  - *Paid*: payment released on the payment date.
- **Vendors** lists the vendor master.

Sessions time out after inactivity; sign in again if you are sent back to the login page.
